# G3.17 Sprint Contract: Live Staff Reservation Creation

**Status:** Implemented; exact-head CI and CTO acceptance required

## Objective

Connect the accepted G3.16 assigned-provider staff reservation command to the
existing live reservation workspace without widening its backend contract or
introducing patient, clinical, payment, or delivery claims.

## Dependencies

- G3.4 assigned-provider and live stock reads;
- G3.5 live reservation reads;
- G3.14 live lifecycle actions; and
- G3.16 staff creation, permission, FEFO, idempotency, and audit authority.

## Accepted boundary

The reservation workspace may create one product hold at a time. It:

1. uses the selected assigned provider and live stock response;
2. accepts only an opaque active tenant-user UUID, one visible product, a
   positive quantity no greater than the displayed available quantity, and a
   future expiry;
3. generates one stable idempotency key when the confirmation opens;
4. requires explicit confirmation and keeps the same key after recoverable
   failures;
5. forwards only the exact G3.16 request through a same-origin BFF;
6. validates the exact correlated receipt; and
7. refreshes authoritative reservation reads after success.

## Explicit exclusions

- user directory/search, names, contact data, patient profiles, prescriptions,
  clinical context, notes, payment, or delivery;
- multi-product form composition, partial allocation, substitution, wait-list,
  override, or back-order policy;
- public/patient self-service, notifications, analytics, or offline mutation;
  and
- any backend, database, permission, or audit-policy change.

## Acceptance evidence

Acceptance requires contract and BFF negative tests, exact receipt correlation,
same-origin and no-store enforcement, workspace success/error/accessibility
tests, formatting, lint, full tests, build, production dependency audit,
database gates, exact-head CI, and synchronized governance records.
