# G3.16 Sprint Contract: Assigned-Provider Staff Reservation Creation

**Status:** Implemented; exact-head CI and CTO acceptance required

## Objective

Expose the reservation aggregate and FEFO hold logic already established by
ADR-005 through one authenticated, permission-protected staff command in the
accepted modular-monolith runtime.

## Dependencies

- S0.3 trusted tenant identity and S0.4 permission/audit enforcement;
- S0.5 batch quantity authority, reservation aggregate, FEFO allocation, and
  serializable transaction rules;
- G3.1 assigned-provider access and G3.3 reservation read/lifecycle contracts;
- G3.7 and G3.10 expiry ownership; and
- G3.11 quarantined-batch exclusion.

## Accepted boundary

`POST /inventory/providers/:providerId/reservations` accepts an active tenant
user identifier, a future expiry timestamp, one to twenty unique product and
positive quantity pairs, and a stable idempotency key. The command:

1. derives tenant, membership, and actor from the authenticated identity;
2. requires live assignment to the provider and
   `inventory.reservations.create`;
3. verifies the reservation subject has an active membership in the same
   tenant without returning identity details;
4. allocates only visible, active, unexpired stock in deterministic FEFO order;
5. creates the reservation, items, allocations, and held-quantity increments in
   one serializable transaction with optimistic batch predicates;
6. records bounded `inventory.reservation.created` audit metadata; and
7. replays only an exactly matching tenant-scoped idempotency command.

## Explicit exclusions

- public or patient-self-service routes;
- patient profile, clinical, prescription, diagnosis, payment, delivery, or
  controlled-medicine data;
- partial allocation, wait-list, substitution, override, or back-order policy;
- notifications, event delivery, gateway routing, or analytics; and
- reservation transitions or expiry ownership already accepted elsewhere.

## Acceptance evidence

Acceptance requires focused DTO, HTTP, FEFO, service, authorization,
idempotency, rollback, real PostgreSQL, migration, and populated-upgrade tests,
plus formatting, lint, full tests, build, production dependency audit, exact-head
CI, and synchronized governance records.
