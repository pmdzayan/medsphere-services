# G3.13 Sprint Contract: Live Damaged-Stock Write-Off

**Status:** Implemented locally; exact-head CI and CTO acceptance required.

**Accepted implementation base:** `85fdf53ce606e8490b9fb988613422efd5bd8f01`

## Objective

Connect the accepted G3.9 completed damaged-stock write-off to the existing
assigned-provider inventory workspace without weakening stock conservation,
provider assignment, permission, optimistic concurrency, idempotency, or audit.

## In scope

- add exact frontend request and receipt contracts for a positive quantity and
  trimmed bounded confirmed-damage reason;
- reject receipts unless `onHandAfter = onHandBefore - quantity`;
- add one same-origin, authenticated, private no-store BFF mutation route;
- show damage recording only for `ACTIVE` batches with available stock;
- bound the browser quantity to currently available units while retaining
  backend authority;
- require explicit confirmation that physical damage already occurred and the
  action records a permanent on-hand write-off without claiming disposal;
- reuse one idempotency key while the confirmation remains open;
- refresh authoritative stock after success and show bounded receipt evidence;
  and
- cover strict parsing, BFF rejection/conflict, conservation, success, refresh,
  and invalid-quantity behavior with tests.

## Authority and safety rules

- The browser never supplies tenant, actor, membership, inventory, product,
  movement, audit, or resulting-version fields.
- The backend remains authoritative for trusted identity, provider assignment,
  permission, batch state and expiry, available quantity, expected version,
  transaction, movement evidence, idempotency, and audit.
- A conflict is not retried with a fabricated version. Staff must reload live
  stock before issuing a new command.
- Damage recording is for an already confirmed physical event. It is not an
  approval, disposal, quarantine, return, refund, or recall workflow.

## Explicit exclusions

- disposal evidence, approval, reversal, quarantine, release, recall, supplier
  return, refund, attachment, or notification;
- patient, public, marketplace, analytics, gateway, or new-service behavior;
- backend permission, schema, migration, worker, or domain-command changes; and
- production or compliance approval.

## Acceptance gate

The sprint may be accepted only when formatting, lint, tests, build, dependency
audit, database migration/drift checks, populated-upgrade verification,
exact-head CI, and CTO review all pass.
